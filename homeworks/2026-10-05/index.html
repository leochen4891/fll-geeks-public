1. Switch to move-by-distance

Self-study how to use move-by-distance blocks, then replace every move-by-rotation block in your program with move-by-distance.

Preferably use cm as the unit.

Tip: check that your robot's real driving distance matches what the block says. Measure it on the mat with a ruler.

2. Use My Blocks

Self-study how to make a “my block” for gyro turns (it’s OK to use one for left and one for right), or for any sequence of blocks you repeat.

It helps tremendously once your program grows larger. Without My Blocks, your program grows so large that every action in the SPIKE app becomes super slow.

3. Work on your assigned mission in the garage

Same as last week: work out a time to come to the garage and work on your assigned missions.

The goal: reliably solve your mission by starting from the launch area, and return to the launch area once finished.

Try different ideas and approaches. Don't get stuck on one method, trying it again and again and hoping it works.
